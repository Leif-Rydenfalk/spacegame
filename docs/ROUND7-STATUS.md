| 8 trees are hard to cut | **done** | Wood takes energy (`physics.js` `WOOD`): about 6 MJ per kg of green wood removed, and a pulsed beam chars the face so a third of it works; a kerf through a 0.5 m trunk is 93 pulses (the same slot in basalt is 7).
